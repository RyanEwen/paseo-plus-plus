# Expand and collapse all

Expand or collapse every visible sidebar group at once. On wide layouts, the two buttons sit in the Workspaces header before the bell. On phones, **Expand all** and **Collapse all** are at the top of the **Display** menu.

Visible means what the current host, project, label and quick text filters render: project headers in Project mode, status groups in Status mode, the **Empty projects** group and the **Pinned** section. Groups the filters hide keep their saved collapse state, so clearing a filter shows them as you left them. Collapse state persists across reloads.

**Expand all** overrides **Expand one project at a time** until you next expand a single project; see [sidebar display options](sidebar-display-options.md).

Turn off **Expand and collapse all** in **Settings → Paseo++** to hide the controls. Turning it off does not change which groups are collapsed.
