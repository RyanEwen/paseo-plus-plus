# Mobile workspace tabs

Switch between workspace tabs directly on your phone with the tab strip beneath the workspace title, enabled by default. Scroll it horizontally, use each tab's actions for renaming or closing, and open another tab from **+**. Turn **Mobile workspace tabs** off in Paseo++ settings to restore the earlier chooser beneath the title while preserving your selected tab and drafts.

Tabs share the available width equally. Long titles are shortened with an ellipsis. Each tab keeps a minimum width of 64 px; when they cannot all fit at that width, the strip scrolls horizontally.

[Feature branch](https://github.com/RyanEwen/paseo-plus-plus/tree/feat/mobile-workspace-tabs). The guide on `main` describes the integrated behavior.
