# Readable tool activity

See clearer descriptions of reading, editing, creating, searching and running scripts across compatible providers. Running, failed and canceled operations keep their status, and full commands and output remain expandable. A multi-file patch keeps its first file's diff and lists the rest under **Additional files**, each with its diff and a link to open it. Group and turn summaries count operations, including failed and canceled attempts, rather than claiming successful changes. Turn this off to restore classic labels, counts and single-file patch details. History is not changed.
